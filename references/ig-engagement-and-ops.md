# Instagram Profile, Story-to-DM & Engagement Operations

> Sub-module for `KITSUNE` covering profile optimization, Story conversion arcs, DM outreach, and human comment/reply triage (paired with `mumu-android-ops.md`).

---

## 1. 12-Part Instagram Profile Audit (`/100`)

Before driving traffic with Reels or outreach, verify every conversion lever on the profile:
1. **Searchable Name Field**: Include niche/outcome keyword next to brand/name (not duplicate handle).
2. **Bio Clarity (3 Lines Max)**: Who you help + concrete outcome -> Proof/credibility -> Single unambiguous CTA.
3. **Single Focused Link**: One primary destination cleanly labeled (avoid bloated link trees with 10 competing buttons).
4. **Highlights & Pinned Three**: Curate 3–4 Highlights (Start Here, Proof/Results, Behind the Scenes, Offer) and pin top 3 posts (Credibility/Origin, Best Value/Reach, Direct Conversion/CTA).
5. **Grid Visual Coherence**: Clean cover typography and instant readability in 3-column mobile view.

---

## 2. Daily Story Sequence & Sticker Funnels

Run 3–5 frame Story arcs instead of random one-off reposts:
- **Frame 1 (Context / Curiosity)**: Visual behind-the-scenes, unfinished build, or contrarian observation (low friction **Poll** or **Emoji Slider** to warm algorithm signals).
- **Frame 2–3 (Proof / Mini-Case)**: Real screen recording, before/after, or problem breakdown.
- **Frame 4 (DM Trigger)**: **Question box** or clear keyword DM sticker moving warm viewers directly into DMs without feeling pushy.

---

## 3. DM Outreach & Reply Triage

- **DM Execution**:
  - **Keyword Delivery**: Deliver promised asset in message 1, then ask 1 qualifying context question.
  - **Warm Engager / Collab Pitch**: Reference something specific they posted/did in Line 1; state clear mutual upside; max 2 polite follow-ups spaced naturally.
- **Comment & Reply Triage**:
  - **Inbound Comments on Own Posts**: Prioritize leads/questions > thoughtful peers > constructive critics; convert high-intent commenters to DM naturally without spammy links.
  - **Outbound Engagement Comments**: Write specific, opinionated 1–2 sentence reactions to the post's core point — never generic *"Great post! 🔥"* bot filler.
