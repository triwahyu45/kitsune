# Kitsune — Autonomous Social & Android Ops

Executes **safe Android emulator Instagram operations** (`MuMu Player 12` + `uiautomator2` + persistent `adb` pipe) paired with **human-sounding copywriting** from `SCRIBE` (`Voice DNA`, hooks, storytelling, anti-AI filter).

---

## Core Workflow

1. **Copywriting & Voice DNA**: Delegate hook, storytelling, Dumbify, and Anti-AI/Voice DNA passes to **`SCRIBE`** (Single Source of Truth).
2. **Android Execution Layer** ([`references/mumu-android-ops.md`](references/mumu-android-ops.md)):
   - Headless VM control via `MuMuManager.exe`, persistent `adb shell` pipe for 80–113 WPM natural typing, DM inbox triage, Story posting/cleanup, and strict UI bounds guardrails against accidental audio/video calls.
3. **Instagram Growth & Funnel Ops** ([`references/ig-engagement-and-ops.md`](references/ig-engagement-and-ops.md)):
   - 12-part profile audit (`/100`), Story-to-DM sticker funnels, outreach/collab DM arcs, and human comment/reply triage.

## Mandatory Self-Audit & Revise-Before-Delivery Gate

Before delivering copy or completing emulator actions:
1. Audit every draft against `SCRIBE` (`Voice DNA` + 6 AI-slop tells); read aloud to verify natural Indonesian/English cadence.
2. After MuMu Android actions, inspect `d.screenshot()` to confirm the exact target screen/message state and zero accidental calls.
